Constraint Diagnosis
03 / 10
Why we capped at 381

The Bottleneck is Creative Supply, Not Budget

Two compounding constraints capped April. Creative supply on Carolien talking heads is the primary limiter. Audience quality on AI Storytelling is the secondary drag. Neither is solved by spending more.

Constraint 1 | Primary

Incumbent Creative Lock on LTO 22

One ad absorbed 89% of LTO 22 spend in April. CPP held at €48 for weeks then doubled to €105 in 7 days as the creative fatigued. With nothing diverse next to it in the auction, Meta could not rotate to alternatives.

  • Tekst 2 - Video Carolien je post je suf took €4,235 of the €4,827 LTO 22 budget
  • Frequency, CTR and CPM all stable across the fatigue period, so this is relevance drift, not classic burn
  • New ABO with fresh talking heads launched 28 April, first read due Tuesday 5 May
Constraint 2 | Secondary

Wrong Audience on AI Storytelling

Lead quality flagged by Nienke. Webshop owners and pre-launch buyers are clicking through and buying the €7 product, but they will never convert to the high-ticket backend. CPP at €23.81 is 1.7x over the new €14 ceiling because the audience is not service-based entrepreneurs.

  • Webshops segment confirmed as a lead-quality contaminant
  • Pre-launch buyers without paying clients are not the BFF avatar
  • Triple-filter approach goes live this week, detailed on slide 4
The strategic implication

More budget without more creatives drives CPP up, not purchases.

Carolien's talking heads consistently drive 80 to 96% of purchases across every campaign on the account. The hero ads in April were 30V_TH_Algoritme on Boost & Convert (€27.27 blended CPP across 3 ad sets) and Video 2 on 30 Posts (€17.50 CPP for 70 purchases). Both were single creatives carrying the weight. Without a steady refresh cadence behind them, every campaign eventually walks into the LTO 22 incumbent lock pattern. The May plan is built around feeding the auction with diverse creative continuously.

Audience Quality Strategy
04 / 10
Lead quality, not just lead volume

The Webshops Exclusion Triple Filter

Meta does not let us exclude buyers by life stage, so the qualifier has to live in three layers. Each layer filters out the segments that buy the LTO but never reach the high-ticket backend. Volume will dip slightly. Quality will rise materially.

L1
Layer 1: Meta-Level Audience Exclusions

Add interest-based and behavioural exclusions directly inside the Advantage+ ad sets. Pairs with the Advantage+ targeting suggestions to keep delivery focused on Dutch and Belgian service-based entrepreneurs.

Exclude: Ecommerce Exclude: Shopify Exclude: WooCommerce Exclude: Online retail Exclude: Webshop owner
L2
Layer 2: Hook-Level Self-Selection in Ad Copy

The first three seconds of every video and the first line of every primary text qualifies the audience before they click. Pre-launch buyers without paying clients self-select out before they cost us a click.

Voor coaches en consultants Service-based ondernemers Met betalende klanten
L3
Layer 3: Lookalike Source Hygiene

Lookalike audiences are built from buyer lists that include the wrong segments today. Cleaned source lists go in this week, then we regenerate the lookalikes manually since Meta does not auto-refresh them when the source updates.

High-ticket buyers only Repeat LTO purchasers Sales call show-ups
Where the filter lives by campaign
  • AI Storytelling: all 3 layers, this is the worst-affected campaign
  • LTO 22: all 3 layers, since it shares the same audience pool
  • Boost & Convert: Layer 1 plus existing copy already qualifies well
  • Viral Reels: Layer 1 only, audience is already converting cleanly
  • 21 May 1-5 method workshop: all 3 layers from day one of launch
The trade and why it is right

Volume will dip 10 to 15% on AI Storytelling and possibly LTO 22 in the first two weeks. Backend sales close rate from those leads should rise enough to offset the front-end dip several times over.

Per Nienke: the trade off is worth it if we get better at selling the backend programmes. The new salesteam needs cleaner leads to ramp on, not more leads.

Implementation timing
All three layers go live across AI Storytelling and LTO 22 by Friday 8 May. Boost & Convert and Viral Reels updated by Monday 11 May. Lookalike rebuild begins once cleaned source lists are confirmed with Carolien on the call this week.

Budget & CPR Guardrails

The discipline that protects the algorithm

Budget Framework & CPR Guardrails

The new salesteam transition means CPR discipline matters more than ever. The 2x ceiling is the floor of the contract. Growing toward 1.5x is the longer arc. Every scaling move respects Meta's learning phase.

Product Breakeven 1.5x Target 2x Ceiling 3x Hard Kill Action at Ceiling

30 Posts Workshop (€11) €11 €16.50 €22 €33 Pause ad, refresh creative
Viral Reels Hooks (€17) €17 €25.50 €34 €51 Pause ad, rotate to Wave 2
LTO 22 (€22) €22 €33 €44 €66 Pause ad, trigger ABO rotation
Boost & Convert (€27) €27 €40.50 €54 €81 Hold budget, refresh hero ad
Safe Scaling Zone

15-20% Increments

The default movement. Every 3-4 days after stable CPR. Example: €280 to €340 to €400 to €480. Risk to learning phase is minimal.

Caution Zone

25-30% Increments

Only on campaigns with 14 plus days of stable history. Expect 24-48 hour CPR adjustment as Meta rebalances. Monitor daily.

Avoid Zone

40%+ Single Move

Triggers full learning phase reset. CPR can spike 50-100% temporarily. Only acceptable when duplicating into a fresh ad set.

The non-negotiables
Daily budgets only, never lifetime budgets on cold campaigns. Duplicate and swap video to introduce new creatives, never overwrite a winner. Maximum 20-25% budget increment in a single move. Pause any ad above the 2x ceiling within 48 hours unless inside a planned learning window.
