Budget Scaling Framework: Algorithm-Safe Growth
CRITICAL RULE: Never Spike Budgets

Budget increases above 20-30% in a single move trigger Meta's learning phase reset, causing CPR spikes and performance instability. We scale incrementally to protect the algorithm.

SAFE SCALING (15-20%)
  • Increase every 3-4 days after stabilization
  • Wait for 48 hours of stable CPR before next increase
  • Example: €150 > €175 > €200 > €230
Risk: Low | CPR Impact: Minimal
MODERATE SCALING (25-30%)
  • Only for proven performers (14+ days data)
  • Expect 24-48 hour learning adjustment
  • Monitor CPR closely during transition
Risk: Medium | CPR Impact: Temporary spike
AGGRESSIVE SCALING (40%+)
  • Avoid unless duplicating to new ad sets
  • Triggers full learning phase reset
  • CPR can spike 50-100% temporarily
Risk: High | CPR Impact: Significant spike
Metric Green - Scale Yellow - Hold Red - Reduce Action Timeline
